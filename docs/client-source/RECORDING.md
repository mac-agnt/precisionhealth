# Stephen Kelly's operations walkthrough (Zoom recording, 2 Oct 2026, 19 min)

Transcribed locally on 7 Oct 2026 and read alongside the screen share. This is how Precision Health runs today, in Stephen's words, plus the fields visible on screen. Real patient and employee names appeared on screen and are deliberately NOT recorded here; the demo uses fictional people only. Client company names are business references and may be used as demo labels.

Where this file and INPUTS.md overlap, both are client material. This file adds the business beyond screening.

## 1. Sales: winning the business

1. Enquiries arrive in **Help Scout** (shared inbox; 15 unassigned that day). Example: IBEC asked for flu vaccinations for 115 employees.
2. Stephen researches the lead with **Perplexity** (a "new business sales" project). It adds the lead to the **Monday.com "New Business Leads"** board and drafts the reply email.
3. New Business Leads board groups: **New contact leads**, **Proposal sent**, then won. Columns: item (company | service | contact), long text (enquiry, source, date, Help Scout conversation link), phone, files, invoice name, invoice email, company details. Views: main table, dashboard, lead qualifier. Related boards: Won Contracts (Occupational Health), Won Contracts (Wellness), Brochures, SLA and DSA Doc MASTER templates, Complete onboarding occ health, **Lapsed Clients: re-engagement**, Sample occ health reports, Marketing material, **Tender Watchdog**, **Repeat Business**.
4. Proposal: an 18-page branded PDF generated by Perplexity from a master document, e.g. "Corporate Health Screening Programme, Proposal for O'Callaghan Collection": company profile, team, deliverables, pricing, commercial approach, upsell packages.
5. Their price list as shown in that proposal (client's own figures):
   - **POC3 programme**: €1,910 per clinical day, up to 25 POC3 appointments a day; unused capacity does not reduce the day rate. Example: 40 to 45 participants = 2 days = €3,820.
   - **Flu add-on**: €15 per vaccine administered; conditional on vaccine supply. Example totals €4,420 (40) and €4,495 (45).
   - **Silver 2**: Silver 1 + atrial fibrillation screen, €3,100 a day, 15 min, up to 25 a day. **Silver 3**: Silver 1 + full 12-lead ECG, €3,700 a day, 20 min, up to 20 a day. (Silver 1 price not visible.)
   - **Gold packages (enhanced screening)**: all Silver 1 components plus extended blood panel (full blood count, uric acid, ferritin, kidney profile). **Gold 1** €3,585 a day (15 min, up to 25); **Gold 2** Gold 1 + atrial fibrillation €3,755 (15 min, up to 25); **Gold 3** Gold 1 + full 12-lead ECG €3,930 (20 min, up to 20).
   - **Executive Health Assessment**: one hour (30 min practitioner + 30 min doctor), tailored biomarker panel, examination and discussion; €575 to €920 per participant; up to 12 a day.
6. Won business moves to the Health Screening workspace.
7. **Recall**: "If I did that screening for J&J on 5 February, I want to hit them next year on 5 February to do it again", and start the conversation about a month before (e.g. 3 March for planning). Repeat business and lapsed-client re-engagement are boards today.

## 2. Screening operations

1. Monday.com **Health Screening** workspace boards: Wellness/Occ Health Screening (duty rota for nurses and paramedics), Current Screening Irish Life, All Screening Information, SISK Screening 2025/2026, Cairn Homes, ABP Health Screening, Staff, Testing 2026, archives by year. Views: main table, calendar, map, timeline, invoicing. Martina and Brenda populate them.
2. **Booking columns** (Wellness/Occ Health Screening, "Bookings Pending 2026"): item (client), date screening, proposals, **screening type** (e.g. Sisk Ire Screen, Information, Bloods, Bespoke, Gold 3, Onsite Ergo Assessment, Exec Health Screen, Bespoke Female, Bespoke Male), **status** (Working on it, Information, Ready to go, Quote in progress), **start time**, **staff onsite**, **staff email**, service, **blood code** (lab panel code written on the blood form so the lab runs that client's tests, e.g. a code per client), client address, **Eircode**, **parking**, **room name**, **contact on the day**, **contact's phone**, attendance links, screening links. Examples: IRFU 1 Dec Gold 3 09:30; a 10-day client in Oct and Nov; Dornan Group bespoke male/female across several sites.
3. Point of care (POC) needs no lab; blood screens need the blood code.
4. **Nurse day brief** (email today, sent by Stephen): "You are booked to carry out Health Screening for: Company, Company contact, Company contact phone, Address, Eircode, Parking, Room name, Start time, Attendance list (live table link, do not print), Screen type (e.g. CARDIAC SCREENING), Screening link (POC screen form), Accommodation (separate confirmation if booked)." Rules in the brief: be there 30 minutes before the first appointment; text Brenda on arrival; if delayed ring Martina so the company can be told; do not leave early unless the company contact confirms all staff were seen (then text Martina, Sinead or Brenda); every screen needs an End of Day form submitted on the day (with the mileage link).

## 3. Clinic day: the POC screen form (nurse, on a laptop, Jotform with conditional logic)

- **Screen type** (cannot be changed once filled): Standard Cardiovascular Risk, Sudden Cardiac Death Risk, BP BMI Cholesterol, BP Only, Other.
- First name, last name, email, mobile (non-Irish option), company list and location, date of birth, ethnicity, sex, diabetes, smoking.
- Risk questions: angina or heart attack in a first-degree relative under 60; chronic kidney disease (stage 3, 4 or 5); atrial fibrillation; on blood pressure treatment; and the rest of the QRISK3 inputs. Sudden cardiac death questions for cardiac screens.
- Measurements: weight (kg, default 80), height (cm, default 170), waist (cm), BMI, systolic and diastolic BP, **bilateral systolic and diastolic** (both arms), **arm span** and **arm span > height** (Marfan screen).
- Point-of-care bloods from the machine on site: cholesterol (mmol/L) and HDL ("if error, write 10 and comment"), cholesterol and HDL outcomes, HbA1c.
- Outcome options per test: Normal, Borderline, Abnormal, Not done, No significant abnormality detected, Other.
- **Pulse oximetry (O2 sats %)**, pulse rate.
- ECG: Done / Not done; ECG result: Normal rhythm (A), Normal rhythm fast heart rate (B), Normal rhythm slow heart rate (F), Slow heart rate and deviating waveform (H), Irregular heart rate (J), and so on.
- Heart age from QRISK ("someone of 40 with undiagnosed diabetes may have a heart age of 55").
- The nurse hits Review; the data goes to Neil and a **unique ID** is generated (POC format, e.g. POC010940). **Stephen wants one unique ID per person that stays with them for as long as they are with Precision Health, and that is the ID they use to log on to the portal.**
- About 25 people per nurse per day.

## 4. End of Day and Mileage form (nurses are not paid until it is submitted)

Fields: date; company/client location; **job completed** (type of work; pay rates differ by job: cardiac screening, flu, POC screening, doctor for occupational health, on-site work); **full or half day**; number of screens completed; **number of DNA** (did not attend); **number of vaccines administered**; **pre-vaccine temperature, intermediate vaccine temperature, post-vaccine temperature** (cold chain); **vaccine batch number** (e.g. "5DE36D2 Exp 05/26", or TBC); **issues, comments and adverse reactions** (note the number of PSAs and FIT kits if applicable); **screening outside Dublin** (mileage claimed; pay calculated from it). Related forms: Subsistence form, Hotel booking form.

## 5. Reporting

- POC submissions land in Jotform Tables ("POC Screen with QRISK", 10,897 entries) with an approval status (In progress) and views per reporter: Reporting (Liz), BP Spiro (Liz), SCD Risk (Liz), POC 1 screen, 12-lead ECG reports, Reporting SCD, **Neil Reporting**. So Liz reports some screen types as well as Neil.
- Clinicians report using **cheat sheets / cheat codes**: a code typed into the report expands to approved advice wording (e.g. for raised cholesterol).
- Wanted: **AI prepares the report and advice, and a clinician still reviews it before anything is released.** "We don't want AI to push this straight away."

## 6. Occupational health

1. Lead (e.g. an aviation business wanting psychological testing and drug testing for flight crew during recruitment) goes to New Business Leads; brochure sent; meeting; service proposal (Perplexity) with a Meddbase appendix.
2. Wanted: **onboard each OH client onto their own portal**, with short videos on using Meddbase: set up, trigger, find a patient, get a report.
3. Client signs up via an **onboarding link** (Jotform "Occupational Health Onboarding Form"): company registration, contact nurse or person, contact email, Meddbase set-up requirements, account and billing details.
4. It lands on the **"Complete onboarding occ health"** board. Columns are owners: **Aidan** (Added to Xero), **Fiona** (service level agreement and data sharing agreement, sent via DocuSign; Stephen thinks these can be generated automatically), **Recurring invoice** (Stephen), **Sinead** (Meddbase set-up), **Fully completed**; plus yearly Meddbase fee and last annual invoice. Groups: Onboarding, Fully completed.
5. **Pre-appointment questionnaires** the client's employees fill before being seen in Meddbase (Jotform today): pre-employment medical questionnaires (per client, e.g. a healthcare staffing company, a food group, nursing/health care), skin surveillance, periodic respiratory sensitisers, night worker assessment, vision screening, commencement of prescribed medications. Wanted: **send these into Meddbase by API**.
6. **Meddbase** holds the OH clinician diaries (e.g. an advisor's day at a client site: 07:30 to 16:30 in a screening van, 15-minute health surveillance slots).

## 7. Irish Life Health partnership

- Precision Health does corporate wellness for Irish Life and its corporate partners (confidential).
- Irish Life books its clients through a **web app Precision Health built** (Irish Life Health booking form; ILH Flu booking form); bookings carry Eircodes and details.
- Board "Current Screening Irish Life": Bookings Pending 2026 with item, date screening, **requested screening** (Cancer screening, Wellness talk, POC 3, Lifestyle Check Point), company, location, Eircode, **invoice number** (e.g. IN26-561), **payment status** (draft needs action, in draft, awaiting payment), end of year archive. A separate **Lifestyle Checkpoint** group (weekly).
- After each screening an **invoice is drafted in Xero**, ready to send.

## 8. Flu

- Monday "Flu" workspace: Irish Life Flu, Precision Flu, **Flu Vaccine Order Records**, archives, contacts, Flu 2026 notes.
- Irish Life Flu board grouped by **week beginning**; columns: item, company, **projected numbers**, date, **actual numbers**, start time, staff onsite, client address. 2025 example weeks: projected 944 / actual 834; 975 / 708; 100 / 93; 950 / 736; 1,280 / 986.
- A **running total of vaccines in stock versus allocated**.
- Precision's own flu bookings are not live yet because there are no flu vaccines in the country yet.
- Clients book projected numbers via booking links; Precision slots them week by week.
